By Day Type

Regular Academic Day

The one-hour lunch break makes HB2 the most practical option for Jum'ah.

Holiday

With more time, head to mosques in Sadar, Mominpura, or Jafar Nagar. Routes are in Getting Around. There's also a mosque near campus.
